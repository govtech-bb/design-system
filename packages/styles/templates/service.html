Get a copy of a birth certificate

Last updated: 12 March 2026

Overview

You can apply for a certified copy of a birth certificate for yourself, your child or another family member.

Certified copies are needed for things like applying for a passport, enrolling in school, or proving your identity.

Before you start

You will need:

  • the full name of the person on the certificate
  • their date and place of birth
  • the names of their parents
  • a valid form of photo ID

How to apply

  1. Complete the online form.
  2. Upload a copy of your ID.
  3. Pay the BBD $25 fee.
  4. Collect your certificate at the Registration Department.
Complete the online form

Other ways to apply

By post

Send a written request, a copy of your ID, and a postal order for BBD $25 to the Registration Department.

In person

Visit the Registration Department in Bridgetown between 8:30am and 4:00pm, Monday to Friday.
