# Inset text

> Highlight a block of important information set apart from the main flow of content.

## Example

```html
<div class="govbb-inset-text">
  <p><strong>This is the estimated amount you may be entitled to.</strong> It is calculated under the Severance Payments Act (Cap. 355A). Your contract of employment may entitle you to more.</p>
  <p>This is not legal advice. Contact the <strong>NIS Severance Payment Department</strong> if you are unsure about your entitlement.</p>
</div>
```

## When to use

To draw attention to a result, calculated figure, or warning that the user needs to absorb before continuing.

## When not to use

For success/error feedback after an action — use an [error summary](../error-summary/README.md) or status banner instead.
